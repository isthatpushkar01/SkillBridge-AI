## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a forward-leaning, tactile vision of AI-driven talent validation and inclusive career mobility. The visual architecture harmonizes three design philosophies:

1. **70% Neumorphic Soft Structure**: Soft extruded surfaces, paired dual directional drop-and-specular shadows, and pressed-inset cavity states that make evaluation metrics and interface controls feel physically tangible.
2. **20% Skeuomorphic Layering**: Glass lenses, subtly beveled physical chips, polished metallic indicators, and fine boundary reflections that give digital credentials weight and permanence.
3. **10% Kinetic Radiance**: Ultra-refined vibrant gradients applied strictly to micro-actions, validation scores, and critical path progressions, preventing soft neutral fatigue.

The atmosphere must feel optimistic, deeply trustworthy, and mathematically precise. It avoids cold corporate sterility by leaning into organic, sculptured interaction surfaces that yield under interaction.

## Colors

The palette operates around light, sculptured chromatic fields underpinned by tinted violet light physics:

- **Surface Base**: `#F3F0FF` (Soft Lavender Tint) acts as the global canvas. Neumorphic elements must share this base tone to achieve proper extruded and debossed illusions.
- **Pure Highlight**: `#FFFFFF` provides the specular crests, directional light sources, and inner glass refractions.
- **Primary Purple (`#7138E8`)**: Drives verified state rings, major interaction targets, and dynamic fills.
- **Deep Purple (`#32116F`)**: Anchor tone for foundational structural frames and high-contrast structural text.
- **Secondary Violet (`#963DF5`)**: Drives elevated telemetry layers, secondary interactive badges, and gradient bridge points.
- **Tertiary Pink Accent (`#E75ACF`)**: Reserved for predictive AI matches, readiness multipliers, and critical achievement highlights.
- **Subdued Tint (`#E9DFFF`)**: Surface floor for inset wells, segmented control tracks, and input beds.
- **Main Text (`#21163B`)**: Ensures strict AAA accessible contrast against `#F3F0FF` without resorting to stark achromatic blacks.
- **Functional Semantics**:
  - Success: `#16B981` (Skill Verified / Benchmark Exceeded)
  - Warning: `#F59E0B` (Skill Gap Identified / Assessment Pending)
  - Error: `#EF4444` (Validation Failed / Action Required)

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional contrast between the tactile, sculptural nature of UI elements and crisp informational clarity:

- **Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers friendly geometric authority with modern rounded apertures. Used across assessment titles, validation metrics, dynamic career path titles, and modal headers.
- **Body & Data Labels (`Inter`)**: Chosen for neutral legibility and rigorous x-height consistency when presenting complex assessment data, benchmark analytics, and technical skill descriptions.
- **Monospace Pairing (Optional Data Overlays)**: For AI confidence intervals and SHA-verified skill hash markers, render in standard tabular numeral variants within Inter using `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.

## Layout & Spacing

Because neumorphic relief requires ample perimeter distance to allow dual light-and-shadow dropoffs to resolve without clipping or muddying adjacent controls, spatial rhythm must remain deliberate:

- **Grid Architecture**: Standard 12-column responsive fluid grid with strict maximum content bounds of `1360px` to maintain visual composure.
- **Form Factor Adaptations**:
  - **Desktop (>= 1200px)**: 12 columns, `gutter-lg` (2rem), `margin-lg` (3.5rem). Ample breathing room around complex dashboard cards and career readiness charts.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns, `gutter` (1.5rem), `margin-md` (2rem). Neumorphic panels collapse from multi-column splits into progressive stacked vertical steps.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `gutter-sm` (1rem), `margin-sm` (1rem). High-elevation extruded cards compress their elevation blur spread by 40% to preserve screen real estate.
- **Rhythm Rules**: Keep a minimum of `space-md` (1rem) between distinct neumorphic elements to prevent opposing shadow vectors from colliding.

## Elevation & Depth

Lighting is anchored at a fixed virtual coordinate: **Top-Left (135 degrees)**. Surfaces take their identity through three distinct physical tiers:

### 1. Extruded Surface (Resting Interactive / Cards)
Constructed with dual opposing drop shadows over `#F3F0FF`:
- Light source: `-8px -8px 16px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`
- Ambient shadow: `8px 8px 18px rgba(113, 56, 232, 0.12), 4px 4px 8px rgba(50, 17, 111, 0.06)`

### 2. High Physical Floating (Modals, Verified Career Badges, Match Hubs)
Pronounced tactile elevation incorporating secondary skeuomorphic rim refraction:
- Outer shadows: `-12px -12px 24px #FFFFFF, 16px 16px 32px rgba(113, 56, 232, 0.16)`
- Edge boundary: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)` simulating high-polish acrylic edge beveling.

### 3. Inset / Debossed Surface (Active Inputs, Toggle Cavities, Track Wells)
Negative depth achieved with dual inner shadows that press the plane inward:
- Inner dark: `inset 4px 4px 8px rgba(50, 17, 111, 0.10)`
- Inner light: `inset -4px -4px 8px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`
- Floor: Solid `#E9DFFF` or translucent lavender tint.

## Shapes

Tactile neumorphism depends on fluid, continuous curvature to disperse lighting gradients smoothly across surfaces:

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Metric Tiles)**: `rounded` (0.5rem / 8px) to `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px). This ensures edge bevels capture highlights without appearing sharp or aggressive.
- **Card Containers & Validation Panels**: `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px), providing smooth corner radiuses that let soft shadow spreads bleed naturally into the base background.
- **Status Indicators, Pill Tags & Toggle Handles**: Full circular pills (`rounded-full`) create a natural, pebble-like tactile experience under finger or pointer interaction.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic**: Base gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #7138E8 0%, #963DF5 50%, #E75ACF 100%)`). Rimmed with a top-lit 1px semi-transparent white highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`). Active state scales to `0.98` with an inset shadow (`inset 0 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2)`).
- **Secondary Tactile**: Background `#F3F0FF` with standard Extruded Surface dual shadows. Text rendered in `#21163B`. Active state transitions smoothly to an Inset Cavity.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Resting state sits in an Inset Well (`inset 3px 3px 6px rgba(50, 17, 111, 0.08), inset -3px -3px 6px #FFFFFF`).
- Focus state activates a 1.5px glowing rim of `#7138E8` alongside a subtle specular inner flare.
- Placeholder text is calibrated in `#21163B` at 45% opacity.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Non-selected chips rest as soft extruded mini-pads (`rounded-full`, padding `space-xs` `space-md`).
- Selected state presses into an inset cavity with a vibrant gradient outline or fills with primary violet, signaling committed choice.

### Cards & Verification Tiles
- Background matches canvas `#F3F0FF`.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` on top/left edges, tapering to `1px solid rgba(113, 56, 232, 0.06)` on bottom/right.
- Internal AI Match or Credential widgets use frosted acrylic layers (backdrop blur `12px` over `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Circular wells debossed into the card surface.
- Unchecked: Inset cavity.
- Checked: Contains an extruded inner gem tinted in `#7138E8` with a central pure white `#FFFFFF` icon glyph or indicator dot.

### Domain-Specific: Skill Validation Radial Meter
- Outer track sits as a debossed circular trench.
- Active progress ring rendered using an angled kinetic conic gradient (`#7138E8` to `#E75ACF`).
- Central hub floats above the track as an extruded circular dial displaying the numeric readiness score in `Plus Jakarta Sans` bold.
